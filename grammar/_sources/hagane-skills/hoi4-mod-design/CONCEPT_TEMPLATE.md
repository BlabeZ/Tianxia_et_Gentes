# [Mod Name] — Concept Brief (fill-in template)

> **This is your INPUT brief** — what you hand to `hoi4-mod-design` to start. After it's processed you get back a polished design doc + a question list.
> **"TBD / still deciding" is a perfectly good answer** for anything not yet decided — blanks become the question list, they don't block. Fill what you know; the more you fill, the faster the design locks.
> Drop this (filled) into your mod's project folder and invoke `hoi4-mod-design`.

---

## 1. The hook
One or two lines: the core **"what if"** and the **divergence point** — when/how your timeline splits from the base mod (or from real history).

## 2. The world / setting
A few paragraphs on the situation at your **main start date**: who the major powers are, what camps/blocs they form, and the **central tension** that drives the mod.

## 3. Base mod & scope
- **Base mod** (if any): which mod is this built on (TNO / KR / TFR / vanilla / standalone)? Submod or standalone?
- **Reuse vs original** — what rides the base mod as-is (tech tree, art/icons, leaders, mechanics) vs what's genuinely new (which nations/storylines get original content)?

## 4. Playable nations
For **each** playable nation:
- **Name** (cosmetic) + tag, or which base-mod nation it maps to
- **Role / identity** in your world (1-2 sentences)
- **Brief history** — how it reached its start-date situation
- **Government type** — democratic / fascist / authoritarian / monarchy / communist / etc. (rough is fine)

## 5. Timeline
Key dates from the divergence → start dates → beyond. Per-nation timelines are welcome. Mark unknowns "TBD".

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| | |

## 6. Start dates
Which dates are playable, their names, and **build priority** (which to finish first).

## 7. Faction / alliance structure
**Who leads which bloc, and who's allied with whom at game start?** (Easy to forget, but it decides the factions on day 1 — don't skip it.)

## 8. Puppets / subjects
Overlord → puppet relationships, with cosmetic names. ("More TBD" is fine.)

## 9. Signature set-pieces
Any specific events, superevents, focus-tree themes, or signature moments you already have in mind — include text / quotes if you have them.

## 10. Leaders & portraits
Named leaders per nation (or "reuse base mod's"). Where do portraits come from — reuse base-mod / vanilla art, or upload your own?

## 11. Map changes
Just country ownership & puppets, or do you also want map/border changes? (Note: start-state ownership is set at whole-state granularity — no province/border splitting.)

## 12. Open questions / not-yet-decided
List anything still TBD. Don't worry about filling everything — this section is expected to have items, and they become the question list.
